The finding

Later in a chase, wickets in hand are worth more than DLS thinks

At DLS par a fair system gives the chasing team about a 50% chance. On average it does: teams on par went on to win 52% of the time. But from over 11 onwards, teams with more wickets in hand won far more often than teams with few left.
